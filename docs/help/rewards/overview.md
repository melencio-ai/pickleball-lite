## How rewards work

Eligible venue owners can earn one free subscription month for an approved honest video testimonial, one month for a 100-day activity streak, and two more months for a 300-day activity streak. Each milestone is awarded once per venue.

## Find your rewards

Open **Rewards** or select the rewards widget. Check activity progress, available credits and the conditions shown in your account.

## Use your credits

Credits are non-transferable, have no cash value, and are applied to eligible subscription access. A Facebook review is optional and not required to earn the testimonial reward.

## Check eligibility

Open **Rewards**. The testimonial submission unlocks after 15 qualifying active days. Each venue can earn this reward once.

## Send your video

Record a short video describing your actual experience. Feedback does not need to be positive. Select **Send video via Messenger** from the Rewards page to send it to Pickle Lite.

## Submit for review

Enter the private reference or Messenger sender and date. Confirm the video was sent and submit. Marketing consent is optional and can be withdrawn. Do not include private player or banking information. An approved testimonial earns one month. Facebook reviews are optional.

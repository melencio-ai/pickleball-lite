## What counts

Use your published venue workspace for qualifying operations while your trial or eligible subscription is active. Authorized staff, manager and owner activity can count toward a single venue day.

## What does not count

Background requests, public booking traffic and notification polling do not count. The streak uses a fixed time zone established on the first active day.

## Keep your progress

Miss a full qualifying day and the current streak resets. Your longest streak and earned credits remain. Tracking starts when the reward program is introduced, not retroactively.

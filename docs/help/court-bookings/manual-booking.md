## Add a booking

1. Open **Calendar** and choose the date.
2. Select an empty slot or **New**, then **Add a booking**.
3. Choose a court, start time and duration.
4. Enter the player's full name and mobile number.
5. Review the details. Select **Confirm booking** or **Hold for QR payment**, depending on the venue's payment setup.

## After saving

The booking appears on the calendar. If payment is required, verify the incoming transfer before confirming payment. Do not mark a booking paid just because the player sent a screenshot.

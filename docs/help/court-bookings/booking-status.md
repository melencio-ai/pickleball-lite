## Common booking states

- **Pending QR:** The slot is held while a QR payment is being verified.
- **Confirmed:** A reservation is recorded. Check the payment status separately.
- **Paid:** Staff verified or recorded payment.
- **Cancelled or expired:** The slot is no longer held by that booking.

## What to do

Open the booking details to see the exact status and available actions. Pending holds usually expire after 20 minutes or earlier when the booking starts. A receipt alone does not confirm a reservation.

## Share your booking page

Copy the venue booking link from **Booking setup** and share it on your Facebook page or with players.

## What the player does

1. Choose a date and how long to play.
2. Choose a start time and available court.
3. Enter their name and mobile number, and accept the required policy.
4. Review the price and confirm. If QR payment is required, pay and send the receipt in Messenger.

## What your staff does

Check the calendar for new bookings and pending payments. Verify paid transfers in the booking details.

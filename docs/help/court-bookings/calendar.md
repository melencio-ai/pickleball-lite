## Open the calendar

Sign in, choose your venue, and open **Calendar**. The columns show courts and the rows show time slots. Change the date with the date controls.

## Work with time slots

Select an available slot or **New** to add a booking, block time or create Open Play. Select an existing entry to view its details and available actions.

## Keep availability accurate

Check the selected date and court before saving. Overlapping reservations and blocked time cannot be booked.

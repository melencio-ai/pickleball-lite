## Find the reservation

Open **Calendar**, select the correct date, and open the booking details.

## Cancel a booking

Select **Cancel booking** and confirm cancellation. The court becomes available again. Follow your venue's cancellation and refund policy.

## Change the date or court

There is no dedicated reschedule action in this release. Confirm the new slot is available, agree the change with the player, and arrange a replacement booking. Cancel the old reservation as appropriate. Reconcile payments separately and avoid collecting the same amount twice.

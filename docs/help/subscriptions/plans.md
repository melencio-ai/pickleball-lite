## Standard prices

- Monthly: ₱1,000 per venue per month.
- Yearly: ₱10,000 per venue per year.

The yearly plan saves ₱2,000 compared with paying monthly for 12 months. Both plans include the same venue website, bookings, Open Play, QR payment workflow and management features.

## Choose a plan

Open **Subscription** from your venue workspace and select the monthly or yearly plan. Arrange payment with Pickle Lite.

## No automatic charges

Account creation, setup and activation are free. Payment is arranged and verified manually. Review the subscription terms before paying.

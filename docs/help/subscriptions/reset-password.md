## Request a reset

Open [the sign-in page](https://app.picklelite.cloud/login) and choose **Forgot password** if it is available. Enter the email for your account.

## Set a new password

Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset email. Use the newest link within its valid period. Choose a strong password of at least 12 characters and sign in again.

## If recovery is unavailable

Contact Pickle Lite support. Never send your existing password or reset link to another person.

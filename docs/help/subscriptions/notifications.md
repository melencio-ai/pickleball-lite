## Open alerts

Select the bell in the desktop workspace or **Alerts** on the mobile bottom navigation. Review booking, payment, Open Play and account notices.

## Manage unread notices

Mark individual messages as read or use **Mark all as read**. Open a notice to go to the relevant page.

## Choose preferences

Open the full notifications page and update the optional categories. Promotional preferences also control optional offer emails. Essential subscription and trial notices remain enabled.

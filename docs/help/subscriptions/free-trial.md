## Start your trial

Create your free account and verify your email address. Your initial 30-day free trial starts after verification. Setup and activation are free.

## Check your trial

Open **Subscription** in your venue workspace to see the current trial status and end date.

## When your trial ends

No automatic charge is made. If your subscription is inactive, new reservations become unavailable while existing booking and account records remain accessible. Choose a paid plan to continue.

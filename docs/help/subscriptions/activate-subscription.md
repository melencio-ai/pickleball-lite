## Choose your plan

1. Sign in as the venue owner.
2. Open **Subscription**.
3. Choose the plan you want.
4. Use **Arrange payment in Messenger** to contact Pickle Lite.

## Confirm activation

Follow the payment instructions from Pickle Lite and keep the payment reference. The team records verified payment and updates your subscription. Check your status and renewal date afterward.

## Keep your venue active

Do not send subscription payment to an unverified account. The player payment QR is for the venue's customer payments, not platform subscription payments.

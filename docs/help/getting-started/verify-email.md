## Verify your email

1. Open the email sent by Pickle Lite.
2. Select the verification link while signed in to the same account.
3. Continue to the venue setup survey.

## If the email is missing

Check your spam or junk folder. Make sure the email you registered with is correct. Use the resend option on the verification page if needed. The link expires, so use the newest email.

## Keep your account safe

Never share your password, verification link or one-time code with other people.

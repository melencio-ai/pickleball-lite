## Before you start

Use an email address you can access. You must be authorized to manage the venue.

## Create your account

1. Go to [Pickle Lite registration](https://app.picklelite.cloud/register).
2. Enter your name, email address and a password of at least 12 characters.
3. Read and accept the required policies.
4. Select **Create free account**.
5. Check your email for the verification link.

## What happens next

There is no setup fee or credit card required. Your 30-day trial starts after you verify your email.

## What you can do

Pickle Lite gives your venue its own public booking website, court reservations, Open Play sign-ups, QR payment instructions and a staff calendar. Players can book without creating a Pickle Lite account.

## How to begin

Create a free owner account, verify your email, and complete the venue setup survey. Share your booking link once the venue is published.

## Who does what

Your venue is responsible for the courts, rates, payments, refunds and customer service. Pickle Lite provides the software.

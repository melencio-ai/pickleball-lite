## Prepare your venue details

Have your venue name, address, contact number, number of courts, opening hours and starting rates ready. If you accept QR payments, prepare an approved merchant QR code and Messenger link.

## Complete the survey

1. Enter venue identity and booking link details.
2. Choose the number of courts.
3. Set opening days and hours.
4. Set an hourly rate and booking durations.
5. Choose pay at venue or merchant QR with Messenger verification.
6. Add your branding if you wish.
7. Review the information and confirm launch.

## Resume later

The setup saves each completed step. Sign in again and select **Resume onboarding** to continue.

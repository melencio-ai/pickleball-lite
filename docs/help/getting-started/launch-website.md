## Before launching

Verify your email, add the required venue details and ensure your courts and hours are set. If QR payments are enabled, complete the QR and Messenger settings.

## Publish the venue

1. Open the onboarding survey.
2. Complete the remaining setup steps.
3. Review the final summary and cancellation policy.
4. Confirm launch.
5. Open **Venue website** to check the live page.

## Check the player experience

Try your booking link in a private browser window. Make sure rates, dates and contact details are correct before sharing it.

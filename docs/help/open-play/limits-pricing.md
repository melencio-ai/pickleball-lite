## Set up limits

When creating Open Play, enter **Maximum players**, **Price per player (PHP)** and skill level. Pick the number of courts needed for the session.

## Review before publishing

Make sure the price and player limit are correct. The player registration page shows session details and available spots. If you need to change an already-published session, review bookings and contact support before cancelling or replacing it.

## Avoid overbooking

Pending payment holds may reserve a player spot temporarily. Check the roster before accepting walk-ins.

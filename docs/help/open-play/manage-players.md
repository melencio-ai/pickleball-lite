## Open the roster

In **Open Play**, select the session. You can also select its calendar entry and view the player roster.

## Add or check in a player

Use the available add-player or walk-in action and enter the name and mobile number. If QR payment is enabled, verify payment before confirming. Use **Check in** for an eligible player who has arrived.

## Manage changes

Use the session's available player actions to cancel or update a registration. Follow your venue's refund policy and keep the roster accurate.

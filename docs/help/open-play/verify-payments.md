## Find the registration

Open the session roster and locate the player with a pending QR payment.

## Verify the payment

1. Open the receipt conversation in Messenger.
2. Check your merchant account for the actual incoming transfer.
3. Match the exact amount and transaction reference.
4. Open **Verify Messenger payment**, enter the details, and confirm only after matching the transfer.

## If a hold expired

Do not promise the player a spot until availability is confirmed. Contact the player and reconcile the transfer separately.

## Roles

- **Owner:** Manages venue settings, subscriptions, staff access and the venue workspace.
- **Manager:** Works on venue operations and can manage supported website settings.
- **Staff:** Works on authorized calendar and Open Play operations.

## Choose carefully

Give each person only the access needed for their work. A platform admin is a separate role and is not a normal venue staff role.

## Switch venues

If you have access to more than one venue, use the workspace switcher to choose the correct venue before updating any records.

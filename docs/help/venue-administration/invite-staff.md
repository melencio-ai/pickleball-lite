## Invite a staff member

1. As venue owner, open **Venue settings > Staff access**.
2. Enter the staff member's email and choose Staff or Manager.
3. Create the invitation.
4. Share the invitation link privately. It expires after 72 hours.

## After they accept

The person signs in to their authorized venue. They should use their own account, not the owner's password.

## Remove access

When a person leaves, use the available staff access removal control and verify that they no longer need any access.

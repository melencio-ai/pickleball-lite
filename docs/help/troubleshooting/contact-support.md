## Before contacting support

Check the relevant guide in this Help Center. Note the page where the issue occurred and the exact error message.

## Message Pickle Lite

Use the **Message Pickle Lite** button below. Include your venue name, the page URL if safe to share, what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot can help if private details are removed.

## For player bookings and refunds

Contact the venue directly using its own website or Messenger. The venue verifies payments and handles refunds. Pickle Lite support handles platform and subscription issues. Never send passwords, banking PINs or one-time codes.

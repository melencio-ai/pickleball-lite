## Check the QR settings

Open **Booking setup > QR payments**. Confirm the base merchant code is valid, your provider supports amount-prefilled QR generation and the Messenger destination is correct.

## Check the payment screen

Try scanning the generated booking QR with a supported bank or wallet app. Check that the amount and recipient are correct. Never pay if the recipient or amount is wrong.

## Get help

If the code is rejected, ask your provider to confirm merchant QR compatibility. Send Pickle Lite a description of the problem, not a password, PIN or one-time code.

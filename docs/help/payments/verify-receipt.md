## Find the booking

Open the calendar and select the pending QR booking. Open Messenger using the booking's link.

## Match the transfer

1. Check the receipt and booking reference.
2. Confirm the money arrived in your own merchant account.
3. Select **Verify Messenger payment**.
4. Enter the received amount and transaction reference.
5. Confirm that the receipt matches the incoming transfer, then select **Confirm payment**.

## Stay safe

Do not rely only on a screenshot. Do not ask for or share bank passwords, PINs or one-time codes.

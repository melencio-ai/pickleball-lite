## Confirm what happened

Ask for the booking reference and check the actual merchant account transaction. Review the booking details and current availability.

## Arrange a solution

If the slot is still free, agree a replacement booking with the player and reconcile the original payment. If it is no longer available, follow your venue's refund or rescheduling policy.

## Avoid duplicate payments

Do not ask the player to pay again until the original transfer is reconciled. The platform cannot reverse a bank or wallet transfer automatically.

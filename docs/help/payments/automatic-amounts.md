## How it works

Pickle Lite calculates the booking or Open Play total and generates a QR code with that amount using your supported merchant base code.

## What players should check

Players scan the QR and review the recipient and exact amount in their bank or wallet app before paying. They send the receipt and booking reference to the venue in Messenger.

## What the platform does not do

Pickle Lite does not receive or automatically verify the transfer. Your venue must check the actual incoming payment and record it in the app.

## Why holds exist

A pending payment reserves the court or player spot while the player pays and your team verifies the receipt.

## Check the countdown

The payment page shows the remaining hold time. A normal hold lasts up to 20 minutes, and may end sooner when the reservation starts. Your staff can see pending QR entries in the calendar.

## When the time runs out

The unpaid hold releases the slot or spot. It is not an automatic refund, and a late receipt does not automatically restore the booking.

## Copy your links

Open **Booking setup > Share your venue**. Copy the website link for the full venue page or the booking link for direct reservations.

## Share with players

Add the URL to your Facebook page, Messenger replies, social media and signage. The links use your venue's own slug on the public booking domain.

## Test your link

Open it in a private browser window on a phone. Check that the venue name, rates, hours and booking steps are correct.

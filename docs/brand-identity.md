# Pickle Lite court identity (Concept B)

The platform uses the approved 40-degree angled pickleball court master assets from the Pickle Lite Logo Kit. The outlined primary and reverse wordmarks and standalone icon live in public/brand. The small-size favicon uses the dedicated thicker-stroke mark from the kit. The platform-logo, platform-mark and platform-favicon Blade components keep branding consistent; brand.css supplies responsive sizing. The existing venue image URL, custom favicon and theme remain authoritative when a venue uploads a logo. Otherwise the court mark is the fallback alongside its venue name. Powered by attribution uses the platform mark only. Authentication uses the reverse wordmark on its dark desktop panel and the primary wordmark on mobile. Transactional email is intentionally plain text (no existing image logo to replace); this change does not alter email transport, deliverability or templates. PWA installation and external social account changes are outside this release.
